About This Event

Merge Weekend is Lakepointe's signature event for anyone exploring faith, looking to take a next step, or wanting to learn what it means to follow Jesus. Whether you've been at Lakepointe for years or it's your first weekend, this event is designed to help you connect with God and with others in a meaningful way.

Join us for a powerful weekend of worship, teaching, and community. Saturday evening kicks off with a special worship experience and message, followed by Sunday morning services where you'll have the opportunity to go deeper. Childcare is available for birth through kindergarten during all sessions.

Registration includes a free event t-shirt, resource packet, and access to all weekend sessions. Lunch is provided on Saturday evening between sessions. An optional small group sign-up is available as part of registration.

What to Expect

Merge Weekend is a casual environment. Come as you are. You'll experience live worship music, engaging teaching, and time for conversation with others on the same journey. Small group breakout sessions are available for those who want to go deeper. Every session is designed to be welcoming regardless of where you are in your faith journey.
